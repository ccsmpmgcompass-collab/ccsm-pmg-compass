## Part A — Correction: four things the audit got wrong

You asked whether the plan takes anything away from what's already built. It did, in four places. Here is what I found on re-inspection, and what each recommendation becomes.

### A1. "Hide Embudo until Tableau sync exists" — **REVERSED**

This was the worst call in the audit. Tableau is not a dead corner of one page; **it is a cross-cutting data source with three live consumers**, and its absence silently degrades two of them:

| Consumer | What it does with Tableau data | What happens without it today |
|---|---|---|
| `07_Embudo_de_Búsqueda.py` | The whole finding funnel | Page `st.stop()`s — visibly empty |
| `app/breakdowns_engine.py:270` → **Desgloses** | **Teaching Pipeline** section, on *every* zone/district/area view | Silently degraded on every scope |
| `02_Metas.py:907` | The **real** mission baptism count for monthly goals | Falls back to the weekly-form `gate` proxy, which the code's own docstring says undercounts badly — **"~18-20 vs an official 41 for one month"** |

And the machinery around it is already built, not hypothetical:

- `app/analytics/finding_funnel.py` — 146 lines, real: a **7-stage person-level funnel** (Referred → Attempted → Contacted → Teaching → Church → Bap Date → Baptized) plus per-area rankings.
- `07_Embudo_de_Búsqueda.py` — 528 lines with a working manual-upload path that already writes to the sheet via `save_dataframe()`, three exports (Rankings CSV, Records CSV, Summary PDF), and date-range presets.
- `_source_caption()` **already handles an `auto:` prefix** on `uploaded_by` — the auto-sync was designed for and never built.
- `get_tableau_daterange()` already parses a `_range:START|END` marker "the scraper stamps into the metadata row."

**Why this matters strategically:** the Tableau Detail export is *one row per person with milestone dates*. That is the only source in the system that can answer a **cohort** question — "of the people we found in June, how many were baptized?" The Panel's §1b conversion rates cannot do this; they are same-window ratios of independent counts, which is the exact flaw I criticized in Referencias (B7). Tableau ingestion doesn't just revive one page — it upgrades the mission's entire conversion story from "ratio of two totals" to "what actually happened to these people."

**Revised:** Embudo stays in the navigation. Building its ingestion is **Phase 3**, the main build-out of this plan.

### A2. "Fold Puntajes › Actividad Diaria into Desgloses" — **REVERSED**

That tab holds three capabilities **nothing else in the app has**:

1. **Multi-metric comparison** — `st.multiselect` at `06_Puntajes.py:1603` charts several nightly metrics on one grouped bar. Panel ⑤ is single-metric (`selectbox`); Desgloses' `{metric} Trend` is single-metric too.
2. **An arbitrary 1–120 day window** — `st.number_input` at `:1528`, default 14. The Panel is hard-wired to 7 days; Desgloses uses fixed KPI periods.
3. **A raw record dump** — the "Raw Daily Records" expander, the only place the underlying `DAILY_LOG` rows are visible.

**Revised:** the tab stays. Only its **Effort Reporting** section changes — and it gets *repointed*, not deleted (see Phase 0.4).

### A3. "Fold Referencias into Panel ⑤" — **REVERSED**

Panel ⑤ shows one metric, mission-wide. Referencias pairs **two** related metrics and breaks them out **by area**. Folding loses both the pairing and the per-area view.

**Revised:** keep the page; fix its arithmetic (B7). The 400%/250% rates are a math bug, not a reason to delete the view.

### A4. "Informes — fix *or fold*" — **narrowed to "fix"**

Informes is the only page in the app with a **week picker**, a **zone scope selector**, and **CSV export of scores and Key Indicators**. Folding it removes the mission's only historical-week report.

**Revised:** fix only (B1 + B3).

### What survives from the audit unchanged

These consolidations remove nothing:

## Part B — The principle this plan runs on

> **Consolidate the source, not the surface.**

The duplication that hurts is **two code paths giving different answers to one question** — effort computed two ways (301 vs 242), Key Indicators computed two ways (Panel says one thing, Informes says "no data"). The duplication that *doesn't* hurt is two places to *look at* the same true number, each cut a different way for a different job.

So: every question gets exactly one function that answers it, in `app/analytics/`. Pages become views over those functions. Nothing gets deleted for being a second view — things get deleted only for being a second, wrong *answer*.

## Part F — Phase 3: Tableau ingestion (the build-out)

> **Rewritten 2026-08-22 against the real exports.** The §3.0 questions below are now **answered** — the user supplied 31 monthly PDFs (Jan 2024 – Jul 2026, no gaps) and a person-level `Detail.xlsx` (92,529 rows × 24 cols, 89,850 people, 2024-01-01 → 2026-08-03, all 10 CCSM zones). Verifying it against `finding_funnel.py` surfaced **five defects that would have failed silently** — they are now steps 3.2a–3.2e and must land before any ingestion.

### 3.0 — What we have (questions closed)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Which view? | **Mission Finding Summary** — exactly the view `get_baptisms_actual()`'s docstring names |
| Export rights? | Yes. PDFs footer: *"Reporting Analyst: Spencer Cowley · Current User: Zackary Barry Butterfield · Certification: Certified"* |
| REST API or scrape? | **Neither, for now.** Manual export works and covers 2.6 years. Automation is a later convenience, not a blocker |
| Export shape? | `.xlsx`, one sheet, one row per person — **not** CSV |

**Two sources, two distinct jobs — do not merge them:**

- **The 31 PDFs → `TABLEAU_BAPTISMS`.** They carry the official, certified *Total People Baptized* per month.
- **`Detail.xlsx` → `TABLEAU_DETAIL`.** Person-level, with all 7 milestone date columns. This is the funnel's source.

⚠️ **The two disagree, systematically, and the PDF is right.** Cross-checking every month: the PDF total is **1,071** baptisms; counting `confirmation_date` in Detail over the same months gives **834** — Detail is short by 22%, and only **1 of 31 months matches exactly**. The gap is huge early (2024-01: 26 vs 1) and small recently (2026: 0 to −6), which is the signature of PMG-app adoption ramping up — a person baptized in 2024 whose finding record predates app tracking has no Detail row. **So `TABLEAU_BAPTISMS` must be parsed from the PDFs, never derived by counting Detail confirmations.** Deriving it would have looked reasonable and been quietly wrong.

**The undercount this fixes is real and current.** `get_baptisms_actual()`'s docstring cites "~18-20 [from the gate proxy] vs an official 41 for one month." The PDFs give **41 for 2025-07** — an exact match — and 2026 runs 19/37/47/44/43/46/47 per month.

### 3.2 — Fix what the real data exposed *(all five precede ingestion)*

**3.2a — `DATA_FLOOR` hides 86% of the dataset.** `finding_funnel.py:10` sets `DATA_FLOOR = date(2026, 5, 19)` with the comment *"the mission's finding data begins May 19, 2026."* **The export disproves it** — data starts 2024-01-01. Under the current floor, only **12,351 of 89,850 people** are selectable; **77,499 are hidden**. Derive the floor from the data (or from `SYSTEM_START_DATE`), don't hardcode a date.

**3.2b — The funnel chart omits the outcome that matters.** `FUNNEL_STAGES` (6 stages, drives the chart) and `_STAGE_COLS` (7 stages, drives the area table) disagree in the same module. The chart stops at *Baptism Date Set* and **never shows the 837 confirmations**; it also omits *Referred* (19,256). One page, two different funnels. Reconcile to one list.

**3.2c — 2,679 junk rows rank #1 in the mission.** Tableau's export carries subtotal/artifact rows with no `full_name` and no zone. `compute_funnel_stage_counts` counts `len(det_df)` for "Found", so they inflate it (92,529 vs the true 89,850), and `build_area_rankings` collects them into an **"Unknown" area that tops the leaderboard with 2,705 found**. Drop rows lacking `full_name`/`event_date_selected` at ingest.

**3.2d — The uploader cannot read this file.** `_process_upload()` calls `pd.read_csv`; the export is `.xlsx`. Add an Excel branch and put **`openpyxl`** in `requirements.txt` (it is currently absent — I installed it into the dev venv to run this analysis, along with `pypdf` for the PDFs; both need a deliberate decision about whether they join the deployed dependency set).

**3.2e — The write will not fit in one call.** `save_dataframe()` does a single `ws.update(rows)`. This export is **2,220,696 cells** — under the 10M-cell sheet cap but far past what one API call will carry. Two fixes, both wanted: **chunk the write**, and **prune the columns** — keeping the 13 the app actually reads cuts it to 1,202,877 cells (**46% smaller**).

⚠️ **3.2f — A privacy decision, not a code change.** Pruning drops `full_name`, and that is also the safer choice: today the Embudo page renders a **Name** column of real investigators beside a *Download Records CSV* button. Ingesting the full export puts **89,850 named individuals and their religious milestone dates** into a Google Sheet the app can export. The PDFs are stamped *"Confidential — For Church Use Only."* **This interacts with the still-open login allowlist** — `auth.py` admits all 97 companion mailboxes by your earlier decision, which was made when the app held only aggregate counts. That decision was sound then; person-level data changes what it means. **Ask before ingesting names.** The funnel, the rankings and every chart work identically without them — nothing on the page aggregates by name.
