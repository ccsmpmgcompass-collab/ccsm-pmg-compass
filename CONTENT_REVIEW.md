### 3. Predicad Mi Evangelio references are chapter-level, never page-level

Every row cites `Capítulo N` plus a one-line topic summary written for this system (not a quoted
chapter title). This avoids guessing at Spanish-edition pagination. If mission leadership prefers page
numbers from the Spanish edition, they can be added to the `PMG_Chapter` column.

### 5. Tone and doctrine calls a mission president should confirm

- **Form of address.** Every message addresses the *companionship* in ustedes-form, warm and direct. Titles are never used ("Elder"/"Hermana" appear nowhere), so a message reads the same to any companionship.
- **No outcome promises.** No message says that doing X will produce a baptism or any other specific result. Growth messages name one concrete next step and stop there.
- **No guilt.** MISSED_DAYS messages in particular are written as concern ("¿Cómo están por allá?"), never as reprimand. If leadership wants a firmer tone for repeated misses, that belongs in the escalation agent, not here.
- **No statistics inside message text.** Messages say "esta semana su área se destacó" rather than quoting numbers; the numbers come from the surrounding email template, which is generated from real data.
- **Names of Deity** used: Dios, Jesucristo, el Salvador, el Señor, el Espíritu Santo.
- **Chilean usage.** "paradero" is used for bus stop (Chilean), and "amigos" is used throughout for those being taught, matching current Church usage. Please confirm both read naturally locally.

#### 10. Fe — La Obra Es de Él

Cada amigo que se enseña en su zona es un hijo de Dios que el Señor ha estado preparando desde mucho antes de que sus misioneros tocaran su puerta. Su labor no es fabricar la conversión — es presentarse, enseñar con el Espíritu, extender invitaciones con fe, y dejar el resultado en manos de Él. Si los números de esta semana no son los que esperaba, llévelo al Señor en oración y pregunte qué ve Él que usted no ve.
